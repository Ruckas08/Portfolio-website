About Me

Lore

I graduated in 2025 from the University of Guelph in Ontario, Canada with a Bachelors of Engineering in mechanical engineering. I grew up in Waterloo, Ontario surrounded by the projects made by students and professors at the University of Waterloo, sealing my fate by encouraging “The Knack”.

“The Knack”, it's a rare condition characterized by an extreme intuition characterized by all things mechanical and electrical.

…and utter social ineptitude.

• Dilbert (1999)

During my time in university, I had the opportunity to explore many fields of engineering by participating in events such as the Ontario Engineering Competition (OEC) and the Canadian Engineering Competition (CEC). During the tail end of my education I took up an interest in semiconductor fabrication and became one of the earliest members of the University of Waterloo branch of HackerFab. I am fortunate to have met and colaberated with so many great engineers through these teams and events.

Since graduating, I have continued my time at Hackerfab as a mentor, in person with the Hackerfab in Waterloo and online with the university of Minnisota Nanofabrication Club. In addition to this regular extracurricular work I have spent time at Sienci Labs as a junior design engineer. my time at Sienci labs offered the complete experience in product design from ideation and prototyping to testing and redesigning to packaging, production, and marketing.
